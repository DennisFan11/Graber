Shared art direction for all GRABER canyon concept images. MATCH THE REAL GAME LOOK shown in the attached screenshots.

Rendering style (from the actual game):
- Flat 2D side view, clean vector shapes, NO painterly texture, NO realistic detail, NO perspective.
- Terrain = flat solid polygons with angular, low-poly silhouettes. Every terrain edge has a hard-stepped inner gradient: 3-4 flat bands going from a light edge color inward to a dark fill (like contour bands / SDF steps). No smooth gradients.
- Background = near-flat deep color; very little texture. Strong silhouettes and value contrast.
- Protagonist (keep exactly this design, small in frame): a dark charcoal rounded-square body with a thin light outline and one round dark eye; two separate dark round hands, each with four small grey rounded finger pads; the hands connect to the body by gooey translucent teal-green metaball "arms" that stretch like slime.
- Light: a warm orange lantern casts soft circular light and long hard polygon shadows from terrain.

Canyon palette by height (apply the flat stepped-band style in every palette):
- Bottom / cave: near-black navy and dark slate, lantern orange as the only warm color.
- Middle cliffs: muted sandstone orange and rust with dusty purple shadows.
- Top: sunrise gold and pale peach sky.
- Pitons = tiny dark iron spikes. Water = flat deep teal-blue bands.

No text, no UI, no logos, no watermarks. 16:9 landscape.
